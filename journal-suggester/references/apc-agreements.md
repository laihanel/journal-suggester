# OA, charges, and institutional agreements

## Separate the publishing route from its cost

Record fully OA, hybrid OA option, subscription route, and repository/self-archiving options separately. For repository routes identify manuscript version, embargo, and license conditions. Check a funder requirement only when relevant; do not assume any repository route meets it. Diamond OA means no author APC, but verify other mandatory fees rather than assuming publication is free.

List APC currency, applicable date or acceptance-year rule, taxes when known, and submission/page/color/overlength or other mandatory charges. A hybrid journal may permit a subscription route without an OA APC; check its charge schedule. "No OA APC" does not mean "no fees." An unknown cost cannot pass a strict cost ceiling.

Keep distinct:

- Public list price for the chosen article type and route.
- Verified discounts or covered APC amount.
- Estimated author-payable total and its assumptions.
- Mandatory charges not covered, with unknown taxes/fees identified.

Do not combine multiple discounts unless the terms permit stacking. Do not treat a waiver request as an approved waiver. For currency conversion, label rate/date and preserve original currency; do not claim budget compliance when important charges remain unknown.

## Optional institutional eligibility check

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing decision-driving inputs:

- Exact institution/campus or affiliated hospital, including country if ambiguous.
- Whether the corresponding author has the required affiliation/status.
- Article type and intended OA route.
- Submission/acceptance timing where relevant to agreement validity.

Institutional email or full identity is usually unnecessary for initial public research. Do not request passwords. Research publicly available library policies first; use authenticated institutional tools only if available and authorized. Do not store a reusable personal profile unless requested.

Inspect both the institution's library policy and the publisher's applicable agreement/title list:

| Condition | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Institution | Exact participating entity, campus, or consortium membership |
| Author | Corresponding/submitting author rules, affiliation timing, eligible status |
| Journal | ISSN/title and covered imprint; hybrid versus fully OA exceptions |
| Article | Covered type and exclusions such as invited content |
| Dates | Agreement term and the operative submission/acceptance/publication date |
| Funding | Full coverage versus discount, caps, exhausted funds, approval requirement |
| Rights | Eligible license and relevant funder conditions |
| Remaining fees | Taxes, page/overlength, color, or other excluded charges |

An agreement may expire before a future acceptance date, and annual caps may be exhausted. Do not extrapolate renewal or fund availability from a prior year. Static public pages cannot establish live quota availability.

Use these conclusions:

- `Apparently eligible`: published conditions match the supplied facts; name pending institutional approval, live quota, and future-date conditions.
- `Ineligible`: identify the specific failed rule with a source.
- `Unresolved`: identify missing or conflicting facts. An agreement found without article-level evidence belongs here.
- `Not assessed`: user did not request or supply an institution.

Reserve `confirmed covered` for article-specific confirmation supplied by the institution/publisher. Never promise zero payable cost from an agreement headline. Provide the relevant library/eligibility link and one concrete question to resolve remaining uncertainty; do not contact anyone automatically.

For examples of rule complexity, see [Saarland University's publisher agreements](https://www.sulb.uni-saarland.de/en/academic-services/open-access-at-saarland-university/open-access-agreements-with-publishers). This is an illustration, not a default institution or a reusable eligibility finding. Recheck the user's actual institution on each request.
